KALIMATI — My English–Arabic Dictionary (version 2.3)

HOW TO PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE (free, about 10 minutes)
The app must be hosted on an https web address once. After that it installs
on the phone and works offline.

Option A – Netlify (easiest)
 1. On a PC, unzip this folder.
 2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up (free).
 3. Drag the unzipped "kalimati" folder onto the page.
 4. Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app
 5. Open that link in Chrome on your Android phone.
 6. Chrome menu (three dots) > "Add to Home screen" > Install.

Option B – GitHub Pages
 1. Create a free GitHub account and a new public repository.
 2. Upload all files from this folder (keep the folder structure).
 3. Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch "main", folder "/ (root)".
 4. Open the https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/ link on the phone and install.

AFTER INSTALLING
 - Your words are saved on the phone only. Use Settings > Backup > Export
   from time to time and before changing phone.
 - Share a word from another app (WhatsApp, browser): select the word >
   Share > Kalimati. The Add screen opens with the word filled in.
 - Sound uses the phone's text-to-speech. For offline sound, install the
   English (US and UK) voice data in Android Settings > Text-to-speech.

NEW IN VERSION 2
 - Voice typing: tap the microphone in Search or in Add a word
   (English or Arabic). Needs internet and Chrome.
 - Practise saying it: on any word, "Check my pronunciation" tells you
   if the phone understood you; "Record myself" lets you compare your
   voice with the correct one.
 - Review modes: Flashcards, Listen & choose, Listen & spell, Mixed.
 - Daily goal, day streak and words added this week (Review screen).
 - Daily reminder: Settings > Daily reminder > Add (opens in your
   calendar and alerts you every day).
 - Share backup straight to Google Drive, email or WhatsApp.
 - Oil & gas and process word pack (225 terms), on the Search screen.

NEW IN VERSION 2.3
 - New look (Option A, clean & light): blue accent, bold modern font,
   home screen with day streak, words due and word count.

NEW IN VERSION 2.2
 - Built-in list extended from 3,322 to 10,095 checked words.

NEW IN VERSION 2.1
 - Add a word from Arabic: type the Arabic meaning first and the app
   suggests matching English words (phone list + online). Tap one.
 - Online lookup (Settings > Online lookup, on by default): when the
   phone has no meaning, the app asks MyMemory for Arabic meanings and
   Free Dictionary for the English meaning, an example sentence and
   the pronunciation. Online results are marked; please check them.
   Only the word you type is sent; your saved words stay on the phone.

UPDATING FROM VERSION 1 OR 2
 Replace the files on your hosting with this folder. Your words stay on
 the phone. Close and reopen the app twice to load the new version.

GOOGLE PLAY (later)
 Use https://www.pwabuilder.com with your hosted link to produce an Android
 package (.aab) for Google Play. A Google Play developer account is needed
 (one-time fee USD 25).

DATA SOURCES
 - Built-in list: 10,095 words with checked Arabic meanings (written for this
   app): 9,870 common English words + 225 oil & gas and process terms.
 - Pronunciation: CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (US English, BSD-style licence).
 - Suggested meanings for other words: FreeDict / Arabeyes English–Arabic
   word list (GPL). These are marked "not checked" in the app.
